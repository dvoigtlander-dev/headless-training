Five Analytics Dashboards

1️⃣
Completion & Status: Track active, completed, and overdue courses by department with trend analysis.
2️⃣
Budget & Cost: Compare spending to allocation, calculate cost-per-employee, measure training ROI.
3️⃣
Engagement & Reach: Monitor unique employees engaged, active learners, total training hours logged.
4️⃣
Effectiveness & Feedback: Display course ratings, pass rates, performance gains after training completion.
5️⃣
Compliance & Expirations: Flag expiring certifications, track renewal deadlines, automated reminders.
